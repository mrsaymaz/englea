Vixar Fight Preview

Try each act with the real fight. Every start is fresh: all four teams at the act’s level, the Class Mission complete, the Rift open, so the fight can be won. Nothing is saved and nothing reaches Google Sheets.

  2. Act II

    Scarlet Vixar

    • 5,600 HP
    • 95 s
    • Level 11 · Mythic

    The Scarlet Brand marks one team for six seconds; part of that team’s damage heals Vixar, so the others carry the attack. A win ends in the second escape and a gold crack.

    Start Act II
  3. Act III

    Gilded Vixar

    • 7,000 HP
    • 110 s
    • Level 12 · Celestial

    At 70% HP Vixar casts the Edict of Separation. Answer the Merge Spell on the board to fuse Slyffindor and Huffleclaw, then break the curse. A win leads straight into the Finale.

    Start Act III

In a fight

Pause, Skip and Exit sit at the top right. Skip jumps to the end of the fight and plays its result.

The Merge Spell

Click an answer on the board. Two right answers from each house fuse the pair; a wrong answer passes the turn to the partner house.

Switching

The Preview button at the top of the board changes the act or the display, and goes back to this page.
