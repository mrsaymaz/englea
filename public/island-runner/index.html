A NEW WAY TO EXPLORE

Three lanes. One adventure.

01

Collect & jump

Follow the gold coins. Move up or down to switch lanes. Jump over obstacles. Each island introduces one special rule. Correct answers and coin streaks build Elemental Focus: a short coin magnet and protection from one hit.

02

Find the answer

Read the prompt first, then choose the correct lane. The gold marker approaches while answer text stays still. Coins and obstacles stay active. You can still jump; your lane selects the answer. No countdown is shown.

03

Face the guardian

At the boss, dodge the marked lanes or jump. Enter the gold lane and stay briefly to fire a coin volley. Correct answers give you a little more firing time. You still need the displayed coin target: bonus points cannot replace coins.

TEACHER SPACE

Make it your lesson.

Reading pace

The run slows while choices approach; coins and obstacles remain active. No countdown is shown.

Soft: gentler speed, 5 guards. Hard: faster speed, 3 guards. Both use the same learning objectives. Soft needs 50–68% of the available coins; Hard needs 70–88%. Each island adds 2 percentage points. The exact coin target appears when the run starts.

Listening and pictures

One listening gate speaks an English word; the team chooses its Turkish meaning. One picture gate shows a drawing; the team chooses the English word. Changes apply from the next run. The teacher remote has the same switches.

Review list

    A missed concept returns in later runs until the class answers it correctly in two different runs. Second-chance answers and Word Trail letters are recorded but do not change this list.

    Test any island

    Preview a later chapter without changing saved progress.

    Curriculum map

    Grades 5–6: Maarif themes, arranged as ten game islands. Grades 7–8: the regular ten-unit English programme. The question bank is original supplementary practice, not a full course or an official MEB assessment. Use Edit questions to match your current lessons.

    Your progress travels with you.

    Progress, question edits and each class’s question history are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Download a backup to keep them safe or move to another computer.

    Local balance notes

    The last 60 runs record coin percentage, missed attacks, Focus uses and completion. No student or class names are included, and these notes are not sent online.

    Class island progress

    Return to Champions and use Save session on the teacher remote to save completed islands, best scores and stars to Google Sheets. Load islands when choosing a class on another device. Question edits and question history stay in this browser; use a backup to move those.

    Stars: finish = 1 · at least 3 correct answers = 2 · at least 5 correct answers = 3. Coin = 10 points. Correct choice = 100 points, plus up to 60 for a continuing answer streak.
