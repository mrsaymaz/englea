# Update to English League v8.0.0

1. Extract this ZIP and use its complete project folder in your usual Netlify deployment process.
2. Keep `public`, `netlify`, and `netlify.toml` in their included locations.
3. Refresh both the board and phone. Confirm the startup badge reads **Animated Evolution · v8.0.0**.

No Google Apps Script update or Google Sheets layout change is needed.

## Classroom check

1. Choose a class and award points to one student: one gold star should appear around their team avatar, and the mission should show **1 / 15**.
2. Award the same student again: participation count increases; the halo and mission remain at one.
3. Select a different student: another star appears and the mission advances.
4. Reset a team: its earned stars and Class Mission progress remain.
5. The fifteenth different student completes the mission and queues the existing Unity celebration and reward. Students can keep earning halo stars afterward.
6. Start New Session to clear stars, student totals and mission progress together.

Undo also undoes the contribution associated with an award. If that was a student's first contribution, their star and mission step are removed.

When resuming an older session, stars are rebuilt from recorded student contributions and unfinished mission progress is recalculated toward 15. Already-completed missions and earned rewards stay completed. The manual mission +1, +5 and Complete controls have been replaced by automatic participation progress; Replay remains.
