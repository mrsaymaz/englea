# Update to English League v8.1.0

1. Extract the ZIP and deploy its complete project folder through your normal Netlify process.
2. Keep `public`, `netlify`, and `netlify.toml` in their included locations.
3. Refresh the board and phone. Confirm **Animated Evolution · v8.1.0** on the startup screen.

No Google Apps Script deployment or Google Sheets layout change is needed.

## Quick check

- On each team card, check the top-right badge: **LEVEL 0**, **LEVEL 3**, etc. There is no ten-step track or /10 denominator. Power-ups remain at the top left.
- Finish a session with contributions from several teams. The League and Battle/Arena champions remain at the top, with their existing crowns and champion titles.
- Beneath them, check **Every Team's Contributors**: four avatars, team names and up to three students per team, with participation counts.
- A team with no contributions shows an empty-state message, not invented names. Equal counts share ranks; alphabetical order selects among ties for the three visible places.
- Narrow screens show fewer columns. Scroll the final screen to reach all teams and Start Session.

Teamwork star halos, the automatic 15-student mission, reset preservation, rosters and Wheel of Subjects behavior remain unchanged.
