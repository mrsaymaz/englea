# v8.1.0 — Recognition for every team

- Added **Every Team's Contributors** beneath the existing League and Battle/Arena champion displays.
- All four team avatars appear with their team names and up to three top contributors, ordered by participation count rather than points.
- Counts are shown next to names. Shared counts share a rank; Turkish alphabetical ordering resolves the three-name display cutoff.
- Teams with fewer than three contributors show only recorded contributors. Empty teams remain visible with a clear message.
- Champion crowns, titles and larger artwork retain their prominence, including the existing Grand Champion presentation.
- Recognition cards use four columns on wide screens, two on medium screens and one on small phones. The results page scrolls vertically.
- Moved each team card's Level badge to the top-right corner, clear of the top-left power-ups.
- Badges show only **LEVEL** and the current number. Removed the ten-segment track and /10 denominator.

Participation stars, the 15-student Class Mission, scoring, reset rules, subject names, wheel timing, roster and Google Sheets integration are retained.
