# v8.0.0 — Teamwork constellations

- Each student's first positive point award lights a star in a halo around their team's current card avatar.
- Stars stay in fixed positions and remain visible through avatar evolutions, point deductions and Team Reset.
- Stars come from existing participation records, so no separate counter can drift out of sync.
- Each different student contributes one Class Mission step; the target is **15 students across all teams**.
- Automatic completion keeps the existing Unity reward and presentation queue. Repeat awards, anonymous team bonuses, deductions and stale manual mission commands cannot add mission steps.
- Halo stars continue accumulating after mission completion.
- The board and remote show the new automatic mission. Replay remains available after completion.
- Undo, restored sessions and New Session keep participation, stars and mission progress consistent.
- Stars have a short appearance animation, with no continuous rotation or pulsing. Light mode and reduced-motion settings disable it.

The v7.2 level badges, Level 5/10 wheel triggers, 20-second wheel results without a countdown, all subject names, rosters, count-based leaderboards and Google Sheets integration are retained. No other proposed Version 8 features were added.
