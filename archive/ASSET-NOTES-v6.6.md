# Guardian component assets

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, using the previously approved Unity Guardian as the edit reference. Four isolated components were extracted from the generated transparent sheet and exported as WebP files. No rectangular clipping is used in the game. Each image has its own natural alpha contour.

Runtime asset paths:
- `public/assets/animated/guardian-body.webp`
- `public/assets/animated/guardian-head.webp`
- `public/assets/animated/guardian-tail.webp`
- `public/assets/animated/guardian-wings.webp`

Final generation prompt:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Production game asset, not a concept poster. Turn the reference Unity Guardian into exactly FOUR SEPARATE transparent animation components on a clean 2x2 sprite sheet. Each component stays completely inside its own equal quadrant, with at least 5% transparent gutter from every quadrant edge. No text, labels, grid, outlines around cells, shadows, background or duplicate creature. TOP LEFT: complete headless bear torso with both strong arms, both legs and paws, green/gold shoulder armor, gold bracers, ivory chest fur and gold chest breastplate with cyan diamond; absolutely NO head, NO mane, NO tail, NO wings. Small natural neck connection hidden beneath a future head overlay. TOP RIGHT: complete lion head with red and gold mane, ears and gold diadem/cyan gem, same three-quarter facing right expression, ending in natural pointed tufts to overlay on torso, NO torso, NO chest armor, NO wings or paws. BOTTOM LEFT: complete long curved green serpent-scale tail with gold edging and red-gold flame tuft, curving left and upward exactly as reference; natural rounded cutout, NO body. BOTTOM RIGHT: pair of large outstretched BLUE feathered wings joined at a small central back bridge, gold/green shoulder roots; NO head, arms, body or tail. Preserve the character identity, colors, outlined cel-shaded RPG creature rendering and perspective of the reference. Reconstruct naturally occluded parts so each isolated component is complete. These will be independently scaled and layered into one assembled guardian in the game. Fill each quadrant well without touching boundaries. True transparent background.
