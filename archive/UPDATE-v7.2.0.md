# Update your existing Netlify site to v7.2.0

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Replace the existing site source with the complete updated `public` folder and the included project files.
3. Deploy using your normal Netlify process.
4. Reload both the smartboard and phone. The opening screen should show **Animated Evolution · v7.2.0**.

The Google Apps Script, TURN function and Netlify environment settings are unchanged. No Apps Script redeployment is needed.

## Quick classroom check

1. Confirm each team card shows a large **LEVEL 0** badge and ten-part level track.
2. Reach Level 5 with a team and confirm the Wheel of Subjects opens. It should not open at Level 3.
3. Let the wheel stop. Confirm the subject remains clearly visible and no countdown appears.
4. Select **Continue** on the smartboard or remote to close it early. Otherwise it closes automatically after about 20 seconds.
5. Confirm the same team receives its next wheel at Level 10.

The names and sections on the Wheel of Subjects are unchanged.
