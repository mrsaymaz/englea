# v7.2.0 — Visible levels and calmer wheel results

- Added a large, high-contrast **LEVEL** badge to every smartboard team card.
- Added a ten-step track so a team's current level is readable visually as well as numerically.
- Added a short one-time badge pulse when a team levels up; Light mode and reduced-motion behavior remain respected.
- Changed automatic Wheel of Subjects milestones from Levels 3, 6 and 9 to Levels **5 and 10**.
- Kept all Wheel of Subjects names and sections unchanged.
- The selected subject now remains visible for **20 seconds after the wheel stops**.
- Added **Continue** controls on both the smartboard and teacher remote for closing the result sooner.
- No countdown is shown on either screen.
- A team reset does not repeat an already-earned Level 5 or Level 10 wheel; New Session clears the milestone history.

Student participation, roster data, scoring, final screens, Google Sheets columns and Apps Script are unchanged.
