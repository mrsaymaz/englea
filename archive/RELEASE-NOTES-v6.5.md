# English League v6.5

## Animated mode artwork

- The redesigned Unity Guardian is now used during Unity Ascension in Animated mode.
- Its body, tail, wings, mane, armour and completed form assemble gradually while the four coloured team streams remain visible.
- The redesigned VIXAR now appears in the secret raid in Animated mode.
- VIXAR keeps the existing combat reactions, hit feedback, phases, health system, team seals and final defeat sequence.
- The redesigned Unity Guardian also descends during VIXAR's final Unity strike.

## Performance balance

- Both illustrations are optimized transparent WebP files rather than live 3D models.
- Only transform and opacity effects are used for their continuous movement.
- Light and Performance modes keep their existing SVG artwork and behaviour.
- Scores, raid damage, timers, evolution rewards, Remote Control and Google Sheets behaviour are unchanged.

## Included fixes

- Retains the v6.4.1 fixed crown position on the Final Battle result.
- Retains the explicit VIXAR curtain dismissal in Animated and Light modes.
